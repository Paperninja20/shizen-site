LeDELAY

A multi-tap delay that dribbles.

Your echoes, on a basketball court. Every repeat is a bounce, and the Echo King dribbles them in time. Lay the pattern out on the bar, or grab the ball and dribble it in yourself.

Two bags

Plain, or deep.

Le BAG picks what kind of delay it is. Everything else, the eras, the drive, the effects, works in both.

BASIC

A normal delay, done properly.

  • Le MODE: Single, Dual or Ping-Pong.
  • Le TIME locked to the host, 1/64 to 8 bars, straight, triplet or dotted, or free in milliseconds.
  • Feedback, Mix, Width and Duck, which pulls the echoes down while you play so they fill the gaps instead.

DEEP

The multi-tap. The repeats are a pattern you draw, the LeHANDLES.

  • Up to 32 handles over two bars, on a grid from 1/64 to a whole bar.
  • LeLOOP sets where the pattern repeats; feedback runs the whole pattern again, once per loop.
  • Every handle has its own level, pan, drive, pitch and seven effects.

LeHANDLES

How it plays.

Drop handles

Click the bar to add a repeat. Drag up for louder, sideways to move it, double-click to take it off. Scroll to zoom in for finer placing, and Le QUANTIZE snaps to the grid or not.

Or dribble it in

Hit REC, then Space on every bounce you want. The bar loops once, the handles land where you played them, and Shift drops a soft one.

Edit every bounce

EDIT opens the editor: an FL-style lane where you paint each handle's Level, Pan, Drive, Pitch (±12 semitones) and the amount of each effect, with the shared settings a click away.

Per handle

Seven effects, on every repeat.

They run in series inside each handle, and only the ones you turn up are built, so a plain pattern costs nothing.

  • Distortion Hard Clip, Fuzz, Fold
  • Chorus Rate, Depth, Delay
  • Phaser Rate, Depth, Stages
  • Flanger Rate, Depth, Feedback
  • Reverb Gym, Arena, Locker Room, Plate
  • Bitcrush Bits, Tone
  • Freq Shift Ring, AM

Plus Drive with a Warm, Hot or Crunchy character, and a pitch shifter per handle.

Three eras

Pick his decade.

Le ERA sets the sound of the repeats. His jersey changes too.

2000s

Tape

Warm and wobbly. Wow and flutter, and the top end rolls off a little more with every pass.

2010s

Analog

Dark and gritty. The repeats thicken up and lose their shine, the way a bucket-brigade does.

2020s

Digital

Clean. Every repeat comes back exactly as it went in, until you tell it not to.

Build him

The controls are on the player.

Click a part of the Echo King and its settings open beside him.

Head

Le HIGH and Le LOW cut. Darken each repeat, or thin it out so the low end stays clean.

Arm

Le DRIVE. Saturation inside the feedback loop, so it adds grit with every repeat.

Ball

Le WOW, Le FLUTTER and Le DIFFUSE, which smears each echo into a wash. At 100 the repeats blur into a reverb.

Jersey

Le ERA. 2000s, 2010s or 2020s.
