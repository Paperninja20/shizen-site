Requirements

macOS
Intel or Apple Silicon. macOS 10.13 High Sierra or later on Intel, macOS 11 Big Sur or later on Apple Silicon. Signed and notarised by Apple — it installs without a Gatekeeper warning.
Windows
Windows 10 or later, 64-bit. The installer is code-signed, and Windows may still show a SmartScreen prompt the first few times it is run — choose More info, then Run anyway. That notice fades as more people install it.
Formats
VST3 on both platforms, and Audio Unit on macOS.
Hosts
Any VST3 or AU host — FL Studio, Ableton Live, Logic Pro, Bitwig, Reaper, Studio One.
Samples
WAV, AIFF, FLAC and Ogg on both platforms; macOS also reads MP3, M4A and CAF. Your own files, or the included library.
